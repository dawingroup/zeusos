# Design Knowledge Base Glossary

## Materials

- **MFC** — Melamine-Faced Chipboard. Factory-laminated board, the standard carcass material.
- **MDF** — Medium-Density Fibreboard. Used for back panels (8mm) and spray-finished components.
- **HPL** — High-Pressure Laminate. Durable surface material for worktops.
- **PVC edge banding** — Polyvinyl chloride tape applied to panel edges. Available in 0.4mm (concealed) and 2mm (visible).
- **Substrate** — The base board material before any finish is applied.

## Construction

- **Carcass** — The structural box of a cabinet, excluding doors and drawers.
- **Cam lock** — Concealed knock-down connector used to join panels at 90°. Standard 15mm diameter.
- **Confirmat screw** — European cabinet assembly screw (7×50mm typical). Direct panel-to-panel joint.
- **Dowel** — Cylindrical wooden pin (8×30mm standard) used for alignment and reinforcement.
- **System 32** — 32mm grid drilling pattern for shelf support holes and hardware mounting.
- **Line boring** — Machine-drilled row of holes on the 32mm system for adjustable shelf supports.
- **Plinth** — Horizontal board at the base of a cabinet, concealing legs/levellers.
- **Top rail** — Horizontal member at the top-front of a base unit, providing rigidity.
- **Rebate** — L-shaped recess cut into a panel edge to receive another component (e.g., back panel).

## Hardware

- **Clip hinge** — Tool-free clip-on hinge (e.g., Blum Clip Top). Separates into mounting plate and hinge arm.
- **Soft close** — Damping mechanism integrated into hinges or drawer slides.
- **Drawer slide** — Mechanical runner for drawer movement. Types: roller, ball-bearing, full-extension (Tandem).
- **TANDEMBOX** — Blum drawer system with integrated metal sides and soft-close.
- **Leg leveller** — Adjustable foot (typically 150mm range) for base unit height adjustment.

## Processes

- **Nesting** — Optimizing part layout on full sheets to minimize waste.
- **Edge banding** — Applying material to exposed panel edges.
- **Boring** — Drilling holes for hardware (hinge cups, shelf pins, cam locks).
- **Full overlay** — Door covers the full carcass front with minimal gaps.

## Dimensions

- **Worktop height** — 870mm (Uganda standard: 720mm carcass + 150mm legs).
- **Splash back zone** — Area between worktop and wall units (typically 480–600mm).
- **Standard module widths** — 150, 200, 250, 300, 350, 400, 450, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900, 1000, 1200mm.
