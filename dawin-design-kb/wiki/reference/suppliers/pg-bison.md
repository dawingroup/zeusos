## Summary

PG Bison is the primary board material supplier for Dawin Finishes. South African manufacturer supplying melamine-faced chipboard (MFC), MDF, and matching PVC edge banding. Materials imported to Uganda via road freight.

## Product Range Used

- **MelaWood MFC:** Standard and moisture-resistant chipboard in 16mm, 18mm, 25mm. Wide décor range.
- **SupaWood MDF:** Raw MDF for spray finishing. 8mm (back panels), 16mm, 18mm, 25mm.
- **FormaWood HPL:** High-pressure laminate for worktops and high-wear surfaces.
- **Edge banding:** Matching PVC edge tape in 0.4mm and 2.0mm, colour-coordinated to MFC décors.

## Lead Times

- Stock items in Kampala warehouse: 1–3 days
- Non-stock MFC décors: 6–8 weeks (import from SA)
- Special orders: 10–12 weeks

## Ordering Notes

- Minimum order for non-stock décors: 10 full sheets
- Full sheet size: 2750 × 1830mm
- Cut-to-size service available but not recommended (workshop optimizes nesting)
