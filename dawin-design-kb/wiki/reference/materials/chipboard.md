## Summary

Chipboard (particleboard) is the primary substrate material used by Dawin Finishes for carcass construction. Sourced predominantly from PG Bison (South Africa). Available in melamine-faced (MFC) and raw formats.

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Density | 620–680 kg/m³ |
| Moisture resistance | Standard (P2) or Moisture Resistant (P5/MR) |
| Common thicknesses | 16mm, 18mm, 25mm |
| Sheet size | 2750 × 1830mm (PG Bison standard) |
| Formaldehyde class | E1 |

## When to Use

- **Standard chipboard (P2):** Interior carcasses, shelves, partitions. Most workshop production.
- **Moisture resistant (P5/MR):** Kitchen sink units, bathroom vanities, any unit near water sources.
- **Raw chipboard:** Only when applying veneer or HPL in-house. Rare — prefer pre-faced MFC.

## Workshop Notes

- Always cut with panel saw blade face-up to minimize chipping on the visible face.
- Edge banding is mandatory on all exposed edges — chipboard core is unattractive and absorbs moisture.
- Screw holding is adequate for cam-lock joints; avoid repeated disassembly.
- MR board can be identified by green-dyed core particles.
