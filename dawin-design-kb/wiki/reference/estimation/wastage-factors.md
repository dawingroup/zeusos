## Summary

Standard wastage factors applied during material estimation. These account for cutting waste, defects, and edge loss. Factors are based on historical workshop data and may be adjusted per-project for unusual configurations.

## Board Material Wastage

| Material | Standard Wastage | High-Waste Scenarios |
|----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| MFC 18mm (carcass) | 8% | 12% (small parts, many cuts) |
| MFC 16mm | 8% | 12% |
| MFC 25mm (worktops) | 10% | 15% (long runs, grain matching) |
| MDF 8mm (back panels) | 5% | 8% |
| Solid timber | 15% | 25% (knots, grain selection) |
| Plywood | 10% | 15% |

## Linear Material Wastage

| Material | Standard Wastage |
|----------|-----------------|
| PVC edge banding 0.4mm | 10% |
| PVC edge banding 2mm | 10% |
| Solid lipping | 15% |
| Aluminium profiles | 5% |

## Hardware Wastage

Hardware is counted as exact quantities (0% wastage) — no cutting involved. However, 2% spare is recommended for site work to cover damage during installation.
