## Summary

Standard ergonomic heights for common furniture types. These are defaults used when no specific client requirements are provided. Dining tables at 750mm, desks at 730mm (slightly lower for keyboard ergonomics), coffee tables at 450mm (seat height). All values can be adjusted based on user anthropometrics.
