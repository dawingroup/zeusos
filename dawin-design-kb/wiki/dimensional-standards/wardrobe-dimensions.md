## Summary

Standard wardrobe dimensions for bedroom furniture. Height typically 2100mm (floor to ceiling in standard rooms is 2400mm, leaving 300mm clearance). Depth 580mm accommodates hangers (standard hanger width ~450mm + clearance). Hanging rail at 1700mm from floor for full-length garments, or double-hang configuration at 1200mm/900mm.
