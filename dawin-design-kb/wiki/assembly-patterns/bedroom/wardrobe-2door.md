## Summary

Standard two-door wardrobe assembly pattern. Tall carcass with fixed top and bottom panels, adjustable shelves, hanging rail, and two hinged doors. Often includes plinth or leg levellers. Back panel is typically 8mm MDF captured in rebates.

## Assembly Sequence

1. Prepare all panels: drill cam-lock holes, edge-band visible edges
2. Assemble box: bottom → sides → top
3. Fit back panel (split panel for tall units > 2400mm)
4. Install hanging rail brackets
5. Install shelf supports
6. Mount doors (3 hinges per door for tall units)
7. Install internal accessories (drawers, pull-out trays if specified)
8. Fit plinth or adjust legs
