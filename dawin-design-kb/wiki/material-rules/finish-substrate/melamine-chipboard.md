## Summary

Melamine-faced chipboard (MFC) is the standard carcass material. The melamine layer is factory-applied and cannot be applied in-workshop. Compatible with all standard chipboard thicknesses. Not compatible with MDF or solid timber substrates — those require different finish systems.
