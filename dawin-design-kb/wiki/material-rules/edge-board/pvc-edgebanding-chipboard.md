## Summary

PVC edge banding is the standard edge treatment for chipboard panels. 0.4mm PVC is used for concealed edges (backs, undersides). 2mm PVC is used for visible edges (fronts, tops). Both require hot-melt adhesive application via edge bander. Not suitable for solid timber or very thin panels.
