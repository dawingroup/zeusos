## Summary

Confirmat (Eurofix) screw, 7mm shaft x 50mm length. One-piece fastener for permanent panel-to-panel joints in chipboard and MDF. Faster than cam locks but visible (requires plastic cap cover). Common for internal carcass joints in economy cabinetry.
