## Summary

Standard 15mm cam lock (Minifix) connector for RTA (ready-to-assemble) cabinet construction. Two-part system: cam housing (15mm cup in panel face) and dowel pin (5mm hole in panel edge). Provides invisible, demountable joint. Standard in all Dawin carcass assembly.
