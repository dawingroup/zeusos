## Summary

Blum TANDEMBOX Antaro full-extension drawer system with integrated BLUMOTION soft-close. Metal-sided drawer system — no separate drawer box sides needed. Available in 270mm to 650mm depths. 30kg load capacity. Requires 13mm clearance per side from internal carcass width.

## Drawer Width Calculation

Internal drawer width = internal carcass width - 26mm (13mm per side for runner clearance)
