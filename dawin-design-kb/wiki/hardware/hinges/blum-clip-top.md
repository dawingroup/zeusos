## Summary

Blum CLIP top 110-degree opening angle hinge for full overlay cabinet doors. Industry standard for kitchen and bedroom cabinetry. 35mm cup boring. Tool-free clip-on mounting to CLIP mounting plate. Integrated soft-close (BLUMOTION) available as option.

## Boring Details

- Cup: 35mm diameter, 13mm depth
- Cup center from door edge: 22.5mm (standard)
- Mounting plate screws: 2x Ø3.5mm pilot holes
- Door overlay adjustable: +/- 2mm via mounting plate
